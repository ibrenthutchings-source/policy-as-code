## 1. Purpose
To ensure accurate, timely processing of employee compensation in compliance with applicable labor laws, maintaining rigorous controls over employee data changes and funds disbursement.

## 2. Policy Statements

### 2.1 Employee Master Data Changes
* All additions, terminations, and updates to employee master data (e.g., base salaries, bank accounts, tax withholdings) must be processed by HR within the HRIS framework.
* Payroll processing staff cannot create new employee profiles or modify compensation rates independently.

### 2.2 Time Tracking & Variances
* Hourly employees must track time through the authorized timekeeping system.
* All timesheets must be electronically approved by the direct supervisor prior to payroll submission. Overtime variances must be documented and flagged for review.

### 2.3 Payroll Review, Reconciliation, & Funding
* Before processing funds transfer, a comprehensive payroll register variance report comparing the current cycle to the previous cycle must be generated.
* A formal review and sign-off on the payroll register must be completed by the Payroll Director or Controller prior to bank funding transmission.
* Monthly bank reconciliations for the dedicated payroll account must be performed by an independent accountant who is not involved in payroll preparation.
