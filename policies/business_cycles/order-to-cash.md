## 1. Purpose
To establish internal controls over the revenue cycle, ensuring accurate billing, credit risk management, and timely cash reconciliation.

## 2. Policy Statements

### 2.1 Customer Master Data & Credit Management
* Creation and modification of customer master records must be performed by the Master Data Team, completely independent of the sales team.
* Credit limits exceeding standard thresholds must be approved by the Credit Manager prior to order fulfillment.

### 2.2 Sales Order Processing & Billing
* Sales orders must be matched against valid, signed customer contracts or purchase orders (POs).
* Invoices must be generated automatically upon proof of shipment or service delivery to ensure complete revenue recognition.

### 2.3 Cash Receipts & Bank Reconciliation
* All incoming payments (ACH, Wire, Check) must be applied to outstanding invoices by an Accounts Receivable (AR) clerk who does not have access to alter customer billing terms or credit limits.
* Bank statements must be reconciled monthly by an accountant with no access to process cash receipts or sales transactions.
