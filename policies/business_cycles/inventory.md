## 1. Purpose
To ensure accurate tracking, valuation, and physical security of company inventory assets.

## 2. Policy Statements

### 2.1 Physical Safeguards & Custody
* Access to inventory storage facilities and warehouses must be restricted to authorized logistics personnel via badge access.
* Logistics personnel responsible for physical custody of inventory cannot modify inventory records in the ERP without management override approval.

### 2.2 Cycle Counts & Physical Inventory
* A full physical inventory count must be performed annually.
* Continuous cycle counting programs must target high-value, high-velocity items monthly.
* Count teams must consist of at least one individual who does not have daily custody of the items being counted.

### 2.3 Valuation & Write-Offs
* Inventory must be valued using standard costing methodologies (e.g., FIFO/Weighted Average) evaluated annually.
* Inventory adjustments or write-offs for obsolescence/damage exceeding $5,000 require written approval from both the Warehouse Manager and the Controller.
