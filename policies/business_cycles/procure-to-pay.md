## 1. Purpose
To govern the acquisition of goods and services, ensuring purchases are authorized, properly received, and accurately paid.

## 2. Policy Statements

### 2.1 Vendor Management
* New vendors must undergo compliance vetting (W-9/W-8BEN collection, OFAC screening) prior to entry into the ERP.
* Vendor setup rights must be restricted to the Procurement Master Data team, completely segregated from staff processing payments or invoices.

### 2.2 Requisition & Purchase Orders
* All corporate purchases exceeding $1,000 require an approved Purchase Requisition and a system-generated Purchase Order (PO).
* Approval authority matrices must enforce strict dollar-value thresholds based on management levels.

### 2.3 Three-Way Matching & Disbursements
* Prior to invoice approval, the ERP system or Accounts Payable (AP) team must perform a **3-way match** comparing:
    1. The original Purchase Order
    2. The Receiving Report / Proof of Delivery
    3. The Vendor Invoice
* Electronic check signing and outbound wire transfers require dual authorization from two designated executive bank signatories for any amount exceeding $10,000.
