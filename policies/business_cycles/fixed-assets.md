## 1. Purpose
To outline criteria for capitalizing assets, applying appropriate depreciation methods, tracking physical location, and governing asset disposal.

## 2. Policy Statements

### 2.1 Capitalization Threshold
* Expenditures for property, plant, equipment, or software with a unit cost exceeding **$5,000** and a useful life of more than one year must be capitalized.
* Items below this threshold must be expensed immediately.

### 2.2 Depreciation & Tracking
* Depreciation must be calculated using the straight-line method based on predefined asset category useful lives (e.g., IT hardware: 3 years; office furniture: 7 years).
* All capitalized assets must be tagged with a unique asset ID and tracked in the Fixed Asset Register (FAR).

### 2.3 Disposals & Physical Verification
* Asset disposals, sales, or retirements must be authorized via a Asset Disposal Form signed by the department head and Finance.
* A physical verification of a sample of fixed assets must be conducted annually to identify missing, damaged, or unrecorded assets.
